# Erode FPO first-visit checklist

For the first 2-3 FPO visits, before any pilot. Goal: learn how the FPO really works today and whether FPOLink would save its members money. Pitch: `docs/PILOT_PITCH.md`. Pilot plan: `docs/PILOT_RUNBOOK.md`.

## Before you go
- **Lead crop:** banana, coconut or tomato (167-185 markets reported them within 3 days when last checked). Turmeric had almost no daily price reports: ask about it, but do not promise turmeric prices.
- **Bring:** the one-page pitch, and a phone showing `@Fpo_Link_Bot` answering a crop name, including a "sell elsewhere after transport" line.
- **Ask to meet** a staff member and 2-3 farmer members, not only the director.
- **Do not collect farmers' phone numbers or names** on this visit. Consent comes at onboarding (DPDP Act).

## Ask the coordinator (write down the exact answers)
1. How many members, which crops, how many acres? How many sell through the FPO?
2. Walk me through last week's sale: who bought, at what price, where, and who decided?
3. How do members learn today's price? Who do they trust: a trader, a phone call, the Uzhavar Sandhai board?
4. How is produce gathered? Who arranges transport and what does it cost per quintal?
5. What records do you keep (paper, Excel, an app) and who writes them?
6. What goes wrong most: low price, late payment, grading disputes, no buyer? Pick the top 3.
7. What phones do members use (smartphone, WhatsApp, Telegram)? Who cannot read text?
8. Who supports you (CBBO, NABARD, SFAC, an NGO) and who pays your running costs? This names the real customer, and the CBBO list we are missing.
9. Would you pay for a tool that fixes problem 6? Who would pay, and what monthly fee feels fair?

## Show and watch (10 minutes)
- Let them send a crop name to the bot in Tamil. Note the words they use for crops (add them as aliases), mistakes in the Tamil, whether they trust the number, and whether "after transport" is understood.
- Ask: "Would this line make you sell somewhere else?" and "Which market would you really use?"

## Record after each visit (one line each)
FPO, district, members, lead crop | top 3 problems | how members get prices | who pays for support | willingness to pay | phone and language notes | crop words to add | objections.

## Choose the pilot FPO if
- One staff member will own the dashboard and use it weekly.
- About 10 members with phones will join (Telegram works today; WhatsApp waits for Meta).
- The lead crop has fresh prices in our feed (check `/prices` that morning).
- A promoter or CBBO relationship exists or is reachable.
- They agree to an end-of-pilot call and to share rupees-per-quintal results.

Drop it if the main problems are not about price or market access, nobody owns the tool, or members have no phones.
