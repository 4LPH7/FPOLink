Why Tomato prices vary across Tamil Nadu

Grown year-round with two main flushes; monsoon supply gaps (Jul–Sep) often spike prices. Local arrivals, transport distance from producing belts (Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra) and variety mix all move the rate between mandis, which is why the range above spans ₹500 to ₹5,000.
